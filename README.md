# 🌺 Deepanshu & Mamta — Royal Digital Wedding Invitation v3.0

An opulent, production-quality Royal Indian wedding invitation website built with HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.

---

## 🌟 What's New in v3.0 (Opulent UI & Interactive Gallery Suite)

1. **✨ Shimmering Gold Foil Aesthetics (`gold-shimmer`):**
   - Headings, couple names, and auspicious symbols glow with moving metallic gold reflections.
   - Auspicious floating Diyas with flickering flame animations flanking the ॐ Ganesh invocation.
   - Floating rose petals paired with twinkling golden fairy dust sparkles.

2. **👑 Royal Jharokha (Palace Window) Couple Portraits:**
   - Deepanshu & Mamta's portraits encased in royal arched Jharokhas with lotus finials and pearl borders.
   - **Custom Photo Upload:** Click **"Change Photo"** on Groom or Bride to upload your own pictures directly from your computer/phone! Custom pictures are saved in `localStorage`.

3. **🎠 Featured Hero Photo Carousel / Slider:**
   - Silky smooth auto-playing showcase with next/prev buttons, touch swipe, and progress dots.

4. **🖼️ Multi-Category Filterable Photo Gallery:**
   - Tabs: **All Photos**, **Pre-Wedding**, **Haldi & Mehendi**, **Sangeet & Baraat**, and **Sacred Vivah**.
   - Masonry layout featuring 12 curated high-resolution moments.
   - Fullscreen **Lightbox Modal** with captions, photo counter, keyboard navigation, and mobile touch gestures.
   - **"Upload Photos" Button:** Allows family and guests to upload additional photos directly to the gallery.

5. **📖 Our Love Story Timeline:**
   - Four-chapter illustrated journey: *The First Glimpse*, *A Promise Forever*, *The Auspicious Roka*, and *The Grand Vivah*.

6. **📜 Interactive Wall of Blessings (Guestbook):**
   - Guests can write heartfelt wishes and prayers that immediately appear live on the Royal Wall of Blessings!

7. **🎶 Dual-Mode Indian Wedding Music:**
   - Tries to play streaming / local Shehnai wedding music.
   - Built-in **Web Audio API traditional Santoor & Flute synthesizer fallback** so soothing auspicious melodies always play when the user clicks **OPEN INVITATION**, even without external files!
   - Floating music player with animated audio equalizer wave bars.

## 🚀 How to View the Website

### Option 1 — XAMPP Local Server
1. The project is placed at `E:\Xampp\htdocs\wedding card\`
2. Start Apache in XAMPP Control Panel
3. Open: **`http://localhost/wedding%20card/`**

### Option 2 — Direct Browser
- Open `index.html` directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
